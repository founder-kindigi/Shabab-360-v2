## Bounded implementation sequence

1. Repair the parks read/create contract without reference-data fallback:
   real loading, empty and failure states; server-scoped city/park writes;
   retain failed form input; test route and client behavior.
2. Define and implement one scoped people-directory contract for staff and
   participants, with bounded search, safe projections and explicit account
   linkage state. Do not return address, guardian or wellbeing fields.
3. Reconcile existing self, guardian and staff profile projections against the
   approved field-visibility policy. Keep profile writes concurrency-safe and
   auditable.
4. Produce a repeatable, dry-run-only Batch 2 import design with stable-key,
   duplicate, ambiguous and historical-assignment handling. No source rows or
   live import are in this task without a separate approved packet.

## Owner decisions that gate dependent workflows

1. Account lifecycle: whether Park Lead can provision, deactivate or relink
   accounts, and which role approves scope/role reassignment. Until decided,
   O01 will not enable account creation, invitations, deactivation or linking.
2. Profile visibility: the approved field matrix for staff, student, guardian,
   community and reporting views. Until decided, restricted and sensitive data
   remains absent from directories and non-owner profiles.
3. Import identity: the owner-approved stable matching keys and an as-of date
   for source age. Until decided, no Batch 2 records will be imported or
   automatically merged.

### Approved safe-default visibility policy — 2026-09-14

Until a fuller matrix replaces it, normal staff and participant directories
may show only name, role/state, and current city/park/group context. They must
not return phone, email, address, date of birth, guardian information,
wellbeing fields, or account reset state. Super Admin is the sole future viewer
of reset state. This does not authorize account creation, linking, invitation,
deactivation, source-data import, or any wider role access.

### Approved HQ park-city selection — 2026-09-14

Super Admin and Program Admin may choose an active city only within the existing
Add Park bottom sheet. The selected `cityId` is required in the park create
request. A City Head receives no selector and the server continues to derive
their assigned city. This is the only approved visual addition for the current
parks repair.
