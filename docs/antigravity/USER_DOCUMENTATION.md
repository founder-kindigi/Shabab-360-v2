# Shabab360 v2 - User Documentation

Welcome to the Shabab360 platform. This system allows you to manage operations, attendance, payments, and communications across all levels of the Shabab organization.

## 1. Getting Started
To access the system, navigate to the main URL. You will be prompted to log in using your registered email and password. Upon your first login, you may be asked to reset your password. The system is designed as a single application, so you will not see page reloads while navigating.

## 2. Roles & Workspaces

The platform experience changes depending on your assigned role:

### Super Admin & Program Admin
- **Workspace:** Admin Workspace
- **Capabilities:** Full system oversight. You can view national metrics, manage cities and parks, oversee large-scale batch deployments, and provision access for other staff members.

### City Head
- **Workspace:** Admin Workspace (City-Scoped)
- **Capabilities:** You manage operations within your designated city. This includes creating and managing local parks, assigning park staff, configuring batches (program cycles), creating groups, and overseeing city-wide fee collections and attendance.

### Park Admin & Park Lead
- **Workspace:** Park Workspace
- **Capabilities:** Focused on the day-to-day operations of a specific park venue.
- **Daily Operations:** Use the mobile-first interface to mark attendance. The system works offline, allowing you to take attendance without internet access and sync it later. You can also view park-level dashboards, record fee payments, and issue receipts.

### Murabbi (Mentor)
- **Workspace:** Park Workspace (Group-Scoped)
- **Capabilities:** You are tied to a specific group of participants (15-20 youths). You can view your group's roster, mark attendance for your group, and track the progress and status of your assigned participants.

### Guardian
- **Workspace:** Guardian Portal
- **Capabilities:** A read-only view of your linked children (participants). You can view their attendance records, payment history, and announcements sent by the park or city leadership.

### Student
- **Workspace:** Student Portal
- **Capabilities:** A read-only view of your personal data, attendance statistics, and direct announcements.

## 3. Core Features

### Offline Attendance
For Park Admins and Murabbis, the attendance marking screen is designed to work even if the internet connection drops. Changes are saved locally on your device and will automatically synchronize with the server once you are back online.

### Scope Selector
Depending on your role, you may have access to multiple cities or parks. Use the "Scope Selector" at the top of the interface to filter your view. Selecting a specific park will update the dashboard, rosters, and financial records to show only data for that park.

### Reports & Exports
Users with appropriate permissions can generate Excel reports for attendance, fees, and admission pipelines. You can save your frequent filter configurations as "Report Presets" for quick access in the future.

### Announcements
City and Park leadership can publish announcements targeting specific audiences (e.g., all staff, specific parks, guardians, or students). Announcements will appear on the dashboard of the targeted users.
