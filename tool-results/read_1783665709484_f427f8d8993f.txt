    11→## 1. Purpose
    12→
    13→This document captures user stories for the Shabab360 project. It is organized by epics and concrete roles so the project can be refined into a smaller, clearer product.
    14→
    15→The stories include existing functionality and refinement stories. Each story should be reviewed and marked as:
    16→
    17→- Keep
    18→- Change
    19→- Merge
    20→- Remove
    21→- Phase 2
    22→
    23→## 2. Story format
    24→
    25→Each story uses:
    26→
    27→```text
    28→As a [specific role],
    29→I want [capability],
    30→so that [business outcome].
    31→```
    32→
    33→Acceptance criteria use Given/When/Then.
    34→
    35→Priority labels:
    36→
    37→| Priority | Meaning |
    38→|---|---|
    39→| P0 | Essential for first usable release |
    40→| P1 | Important for daily operations |
    41→| P2 | Useful but can wait |
    42→| P3 | Optional or likely phase 2 |
    43→
    44→Status labels:
    45→
    46→| Status | Meaning |
    47→|---|---|
    48→| Implemented | Exists in current product |
    49→| Needs Refinement | Exists but must be simplified or clarified |
    50→| Proposed | Needed for refinement but not necessarily implemented |
    51→| To Confirm | Business decision required |
    52→
    53→## 3. Personas
    54→
    55→| Persona | Description |
    56→|---|---|
    57→| Public Family Applicant | Family member applying for admission |
    58→| Super Admin | Technical recovery/full-access administrator |
    59→| Program Admin | HQ or Markazi Masoul operator |
    60→| City Head | City Masoul operator |
    61→| Park Admin | Park-level operations user |
    62→| Park Lead | Park-level lead with attendance responsibility |
    63→| Murabbi | Mentor working with one group or park |
    64→| Guardian | Parent/guardian linked to Shabab |
    65→| Student | Shabab participant |

    67→## 4. Epic 1: Authentication and Role Access
    68→
    69→### US-AUTH-001: Login With Issued Credentials
    70→
    71→As an internal user, I want to log in using credentials issued by an administrator, so that I can access my assigned workspace.
    72→
    73→Priority: P0  
    74→Status: Implemented
    75→
    76→Acceptance criteria:
    77→
    78→- Given I have a valid auth account, when I enter my email and password, then I am signed in.
    79→- Given my credentials are invalid, when I submit the form, then I see a login error.
    80→- Given I am not authenticated, when I open a protected route, then I am redirected to `/login`.
    81→
    82→### US-AUTH-002: Role-Based Landing
    83→
    84→As an authenticated user, I want the system to send me to the correct workspace, so that I do not need to choose my role manually.
    85→
    86→Priority: P0  
    87→Status: Implemented
    88→
    89→Acceptance criteria:
    90→
    91→- Given I am a Program Admin, when I sign in, then I land on `/admin`.
    92→- Given I am a City Head, when I sign in, then I land on `/admin`.
    93→- Given I am a Park Admin, Park Lead, or Murabbi, when I sign in, then I land on `/park`.
    94→- Given I am a Guardian, when I sign in, then I land on `/guardian`.
    95→- Given I am a Student, when I sign in, then I land on `/student`.
    96→
    97→### US-AUTH-003: First Login Password Reset
    98→
    99→As a newly provisioned user, I want to be asked to reset my temporary password, so that my account is not left with a shared temporary password.
   100→
   101→Priority: P1  
   102→Status: Implemented
   103→
   104→Acceptance criteria:
   105→
   106→- Given my account is marked `must_reset_password`, when I log in, then I am redirected to `/reset-password`.
   107→- Given I submit matching valid passwords, when reset succeeds, then the reset flag is cleared.
   108→- Given my new password and confirmation do not match, when I submit, then I see a validation error.
   109→
   110→### US-AUTH-004: Access Pending State
   111→
   112→As an authenticated but unlinked user, I want to see an access pending page, so that I know my account exists but is not ready.
   113→
   114→Priority: P1  
   115→Status: Implemented
   116→
   117→Acceptance criteria:
   118→
   119→- Given my auth account has no linked role/profile, when I sign in, then I land on `/access-pending`.
   120→- Given I am on access pending, when I sign out, then I return to login.
   121→
   122→### US-AUTH-005: Finalize Unlinked Account Policy
   123→
   124→As a project owner, I want a clear policy for unlinked auth accounts, so that the system is not confusing or insecure.
   125→
   126→Priority: P0  
   127→Status: Proposed
   128→
   129→Acceptance criteria:
   130→
   131→- Given an auth account is unlinked, when it signs in, then the expected behavior is documented.
   132→- Given unlinked accounts are not allowed, when one exists, then admins can identify and resolve it.
   133→- Given the policy is approved, then the access workspace and UAT checklist reflect it.
